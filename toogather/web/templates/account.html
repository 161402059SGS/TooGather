Your name

This is the name on everything you record. Changing it changes how you are shown everywhere, including on things you wrote before - it is the same account, under a new name.

How you sign in

There is nothing to change here. TooGather has no passwords: this browser holds a signed cookie that says who you are, and your project roles hang off that. Signing out on this browser and typing your name again creates a new person, not the same one - so if you are moving to another machine, ask a project owner for an invite link rather than retyping your name.

Switch this account off

Deactivating signs you out and stops this account being used. What you wrote stays, with your name on it, because removing it would blank out the history it is part of. An administrator can switch it back on.
